## §1 Clock-Ratio Determination (Methods)

> **Figure**: Figure 1
> ([fig1_ratio_segments.png](../clock_ratio/paper_figs/fig1_ratio_segments.png)),
> per-segment deviations $y_i$ of $R_i$.

Two optical clocks, one in Wuhan (Yb) and one in Shanghai (Sr), are compared remotely over
a 1550 nm fibre link. Let $b_i(t)$ be the longest jump-free beat-note series of segment $i$
and $m$ the global median. The in-segment beat deviation is

$$\overline{\delta b}_i \;=\; \overline{b_i} - m .$$

After the Sr-side systematic shift $a_i$ and the Yb-side shift $b_{\mathrm{Yb}}$ are combined
into the Dr coefficient, the Yb/Sr ratio of segment $i$ follows from the full inversion

$$R_i \;=\; \bigl[\,\mathrm{ratio\_base}_i + \mathrm{Dr}_i\,\bigr]^{-1}\times(1+\delta_g),
\qquad \delta_g=-3.116\times10^{-15},$$

computed throughout at 80-decimal precision (`Decimal80`, mirroring MATLAB `vpa(...,80)`),
so that differences at the $10^{-18}$ level are not destroyed by double-precision rounding
(float64 absolute precision is about $2.6\times10^{-16}$). The duration-weighted centre of
the whole experiment is

$$R_{\text{duration}} \;=\; \frac{\sum_i n_i R_i}{\sum_i n_i},
\qquad \sum_i n_i = 1{,}008{,}912,$$

giving the baseline centre $R_{\text{duration}} = 1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,720\,369\,6$
(no tidal correction).

> *Source: `clock_ratio/ratio_17seg.csv`, `clock_ratio/tidal_correction/summary.json`
> (raw scenario).* Figure 1 shows the duration-weighted mean of the 17 segment deviations
> $y_i=R_i/R_{\text{seg1}}-1$ to be $-0.482\times10^{-18}$ (arithmetic mean
> $+0.102\times10^{-18}$), with inter-segment scatter $\sigma(y_i)=2.98\times10^{-18}$.

## §2 Tidal Gravitational-Redshift Detection (Results)

> **Figure**: Figure 2
> ([fig2_tidal_detection.png](../clock_ratio/paper_figs/fig2_tidal_detection.png)),
> per-segment tidal-amplitude fits $A_i$.

The tide (solid Earth tide plus ocean loading) changes the geopotential difference between
the two sites, $\Delta W(t)=W_{\text{WUHN}}(t)-W_{\text{SHAO}}(t)$, which through the
general-relativistic gravitational redshift leaves an imprint on the comparison frequency:

$$\frac{\Delta f}{f} \;=\; \frac{\Delta W(t)}{c^{2}},$$

with an rms amplitude of about $4.8\times10^{-18}$. After projection onto a 1200 s
triangular window within each segment, the single-segment signal-to-noise ratio is only
$\mathrm{SNR}\approx0.28$, so no individual segment is significant (Figure 2). Across
segments, however, the sign agreement is significant:

$$14/17\ \text{segments same sign}\ (p=0.013),\qquad
\text{Stouffer }|z|=5.87\ (p=4.3\times10^{-9}),\qquad
\text{Fisher }p=2.2\times10^{-6}.$$

The fitted amplitude is $A=-0.54\pm0.08$ (about $6.4\sigma$ from zero): the tidal signal is
detected **with the correct sign and at roughly half the theoretical amplitude**. At the
segment-mean level, $y_i$ is positively correlated with the session tidal shift
$\Delta f/f$, with Pearson $r=+0.518\ (p=0.033)$.

> *Source: `clock/segment_analysis/batch_aggregate.csv`,
> `clock_ratio/correlation_reanalysis.csv`.*

## §3 Validity and Magnitude of the Tidal Correction

> **Figures**: Figure 3
> ([fig3_correction.png](../clock_ratio/paper_figs/fig3_correction.png)), long-term
> stability and inter-group consistency; Figure 4
> ([fig4_methods_summary.png](../clock_ratio/paper_figs/fig4_methods_summary.png)),
> the four method centres.

The rationale for the tidal correction is that **the corrected residual should no longer
correlate systematically with the tidal template**. We adopt two fixed-response scenarios
rather than refitting:

$$b_{\text{corr}}(t)=b_{\text{raw}}(t)-A\,h(t),\qquad
h(t)=F_{1550}\,\frac{\Delta W(t)}{c^{2}},\qquad
F_{1550}=N_{1550}^{\text{WH}}f_{\text{rep}}=193.3992\ \text{THz},$$

where `theory` takes $A=-1$ (full trust in the professional tidal data and in relativistic
gravitation) and `empirical` takes $A=-0.54$ (trust the waveform, use the historical fitted
amplitude).

**Validity (direction self-check).** After correcting with $A_{\text{fix}}=-0.54$, the
segment-mean correlation between residual and template collapses from $-0.134$ to $+0.003$,
the signed Stouffer $z$ drops from $-5.16$ to $+0.39\ (p=0.69)$, and the number of
negatively correlated segments falls to $8/17$ (random, $p=1.000$); a scan over
$A_{\text{fix}}$ places the zero of the residual correlation exactly at $-0.54$, consistent
with the historical precision-weighted amplitude $-0.5397\pm0.084$. This confirms directly,
from the disappearance of the post-correction correlation, that the **sign and magnitude of
$A=-0.54$ are both correct**, and it does not rely on the not-yet-fixed comb/local-oscillator
sign factor $s_{\text{beat}}$.

**Magnitude.** The correction shifts the duration-weighted centre of the whole experiment up
from the baseline $1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,720\,37$:

| Scenario | $A$ | $R_{\text{duration}}$ | Difference vs baseline ($10^{-18}$) |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw | 0 | $1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,720\,37$ | 0 |
| theory | $-1$ | $1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,720\,81$ | $+0.44$ |
| empirical | $-0.54$ | $1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,720\,61$ | $+0.24$ |

Against the experiment's own point-by-point tidal-corrected reference
$1.207\,507\,039\,343\,337\,721\,3(23)$, the deviation (**duration-weighted convention**)
converges from the baseline $-0.93\times10^{-18}$ to $-0.49\times10^{-18}$ for `theory` and
$-0.69\times10^{-18}$ for `empirical` (both move toward the reference). Under the
**precision-weighted WLS centre** (weights $1/u_i^2$, the same convention as the experiment),
the deviations are baseline $-0.50$, `theory` $+0.39$, and `empirical` $-0.04\times10^{-18}$,
i.e. `empirical` is closest to the reference. Inter-group consistency is measured by
$\chi^2_{\text{red}}$: baseline $5.42\to$ `theory` $3.70$ ($-31.8\%$), `empirical` $4.56$
($-15.9\%$); the likelihood ratio $\Delta\chi^2=27.6$ (dof$=1$, $p=1.5\times10^{-7}$, about
$5.3\sigma$) shows that the compensation improves inter-group consistency **highly
significantly**. The four statistical combination methods (WLS / Birge / Mandel–Paule /
Bayesian) give a consistent direction of change (Figure 4).

> `clock_ratio/statistical_methods_tidal.json`; direction self-check in
> [METHODOLOGY §9.5](METHODOLOGY.md).*

## §4 Caveats and Validity Boundaries

These must be stated honestly in the paper; they are a source of credibility, not a defect.

1. **$u_i$ is unreliable.** Each segment's statistical uncertainty comes from an OADEV
   extrapolation ($128$–$T/4$ s); the flicker floor makes all segments systematically
   underestimate by about 30–40%, compounded by anomalous individual segments (5/6/16).
   The combined values are therefore for method comparison only and are **not a final
   uncertainty statement**.
2. **The DC assumption behind $A=-0.54$.** This amplitude comes from a historical fit to the
   **demeaned waveform**; applying it to the DC (mean) part of an undemeaned template is an
   additional assumption, not a calibration.
3. **Segment-9 sensitivity.** After removing segment 9 (whose `shift_a` is anomalous), the
   effect of tidal compensation in "reducing $\chi^2_{\text{red}}$" weakens sharply or even
   reverses sign (`theory` relative to baseline goes from $+31.8\%$ to $-5.1\%$), i.e. the
   improvement **depends heavily on segment 9** and must be interpreted cautiously until the
   reality of segment 9's `shift_a` is settled.
4. **Fixed sign.** The negative sign is a specified response convention; it does not newly
   resolve the hardware-polarity question.
